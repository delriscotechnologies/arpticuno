# Code of conduct

Contributors must communicate professionally and treat others with respect.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publication of private network information, and attempts to pressure maintainers into unsafe releases are not acceptable.

Security reports must follow `SECURITY.md` and must not be disclosed publicly before maintainers have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate them.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.
